Set up your firstmate

Your firstmate runs on the provider and model you choose, with your own API key. A key is checked with its provider, stored encrypted, and never shown again, not even to you or the admin.

GitHub token (optional)

A fine-grained personal access token lets your firstmate push branches and open pull requests. Without one it works but cannot reach GitHub as you.

4. Your firstmate

Changing your key or model restarts your firstmate; work in flight restarts with it.
